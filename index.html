    How it moves

    • The liquid churns hardest around the hands, most of all at their tips.
    • In the last ten seconds of every minute it builds, peaks as the minute turns, then settles.
    • Through the minute before each hour, the liquid starts turning counterclockwise around the hands, small at the centre at first, then growing out to the minute ring. Through the minute after, it unwinds clockwise from the edge back to the centre. You can turn it off with On the hour, under Moments in Settings.
    • Midnight is different. Through the last minute, all the colour is drawn, spiralling counterclockwise, into a small knot at the centre, leaving the clock bare. At 12:00 everything stops dead for a few seconds. Then the knot lets go and the colours spin slowly back out across the screen, in a new mix for the new day.
    • Second hand, under Clock in Settings, adds a thin second hand in the palette's tips colour. It sweeps smoothly and leaves a faint wake in the liquid.
    • Parallax, under Liquid in Settings, lifts the hands off the liquid. Tilt the phone and they shift over it with their shadow. Deep, also under Liquid, turns the colours into a raised, glossy surface that catches the light as you tilt. On a computer, move the mouse instead.
    • Drag a finger across the liquid to stir it. The colours swirl after your finger, then slowly settle.
    • Touch the centre of the clock to open Settings and About. Nothing else is a button.

    Keeping it on display

    Added to your Home Screen or Dock, the clock opens full screen, without the browser's bars. Turn the phone sideways on a stand.

    After your first touch the clock asks the screen to stay on. If your phone still dims, set Auto-Lock (iPhone) or Screen timeout (Android) to a longer time while the clock is on display.

    Once it has opened, the clock keeps working without a connection.

    If your phone is set to reduce motion, the liquid stays calmer, the big moments are gentler, and Parallax and Deep hold still.

    Private by design

    No account, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sent anywhere. Your settings are remembered in this browser only. With Parallax or Deep on, the phone's tilt is read to move the picture and never leaves it.
